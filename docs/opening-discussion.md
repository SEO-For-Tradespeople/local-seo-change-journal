# Try SEO Enquiry Tracker & Monthly Planner and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for trade businesses investing in ongoing SEO. Record enquiries, quotes and jobs, compare a previous month and decide what to work on next.

[Open the tool](https://seo-for-tradespeople.github.io/local-seo-change-journal/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. A simple monthly review you can save alongside your work records.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

Monthly counts can change for many reasons. This tool does not connect to analytics, identify an acquisition source or attribute results to SEO.

Maintained by [SEO for Tradespeople](https://seofortradespeople.co.uk/), operated by JW Digital Services Ltd. The original developer helpers are still available in the repository, but they are not needed to use the browser tool.
